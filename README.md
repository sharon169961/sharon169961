## About Me

I'm an Electronics and Computer Engineering student interested in developing intelligent systems that bridge the gap between hardware and software.

My work and interests span:

- **Robotics:** ROS 2, Gazebo, autonomous navigation and robot control.
- **Embedded Systems:** ESP32, Arduino, microcontrollers and sensor integration.
- **Automotive Electronics:** Vehicle control systems, mixed-signal electronics and PCB design.
- **Digital Systems:** Logic design, computer architecture and VLSI fundamentals.
- **AI & Computer Vision:** Python, OpenCV and intelligent perception systems.

I enjoy working on projects that involve building, integrating and testing real engineering systems.
